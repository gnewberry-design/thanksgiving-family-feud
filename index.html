Teacher-controlled game: share this browser tab in your live class. Teams respond aloud or in chat.

🎤 How to Play

  1. Split the class into two teams and rename them above. Share your screen in your live class.
  2. Click 🔐 Teacher Answer Key to open a separate host-only window. Share only the game tab so students cannot see the key.
  3. Read the prompt aloud. Teams take turns guessing the most popular answers; click a numbered tile to reveal a correct answer and its points.
  4. Click Strike for an incorrect guess. After three strikes, let the other team attempt one steal guess.
  5. Click Award Round under the winning team to transfer the revealed answer points. A round can be awarded only once.
  6. Click Next for another round. The team with the most points wins!

Note: Answer point values are made-up classroom game values, not real survey results. For consistent scoring, reveal remaining answers after a successful steal before awarding if you want all round points counted.
